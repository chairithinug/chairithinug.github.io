Reflections on AI and Coding

October 17, 2025 • 5 min read • Back to articles
AI productivity coding

TL;DR

  • Used AI tools like ChatGPT and GitHub Copilot to develop a personal website efficiently.
  • Recognized that building a website requires understanding of web design fundamentals (HTML, CSS, JavaScript).
  • AI tools accelerate coding for simple tasks, but complex projects need human guidance.
  • Future use of AI tools will be thoughtful; they enhance efficiency but are not substitutes for human effort.

I have a strong background in machine learning and artificial intelligence. After completing my studies at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, I began working at Kasikorn Business-Technology Group, Kasikorn Labs, a subsidiary of Kasikorn Bank in Thailand. My primary role there was to read research papers and apply the ideas from those studies, particularly in the areas of face recognition and image quality assessment.

It was not until I attended the University of Copenhagen for my master's degree that I began using ChatGPT, a tool developed by OpenAI, to help me with specific coding tasks like debugging. I started using it more frequently while working as a student assistant at the Royal Danish Library, where I was involved in a project to create a digital archive.

After completing my master's degree, I began working on my second personal website, which you are now visiting. I utilized AI tools such as ChatGPT and GitHub Copilot to develop the site more efficiently. I was impressed with the results, but realized that building a well-functioning and visually appealing website requires a step-by-step approach.

Will I continue to use AI tools in the future? I think so, but I will do so thoughtfully. AI provides tools to enhance coding efficiency, rather than being a complete substitute for human effort.
