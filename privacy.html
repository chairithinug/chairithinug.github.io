Privacy and Accessibility

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 27 April 2026

This site (chairithinug.com) is a personal portfolio operated by Anapat Chairithinugull. It does not collect personal data unless you opt in to analytics via the cookie banner. This page describes what is collected, why, and how to opt out — under both the Thailand Personal Data Protection Act (PDPA) and the EU/UK GDPR.

What is collected

  • Without consent: nothing beyond what your browser sends to GitHub Pages (IP, user agent, request path) for routing the page.
  • After accepting analytics: Google Analytics 4 (GA4) measures anonymized page views, click events on resume downloads and outbound links, and aggregated session data. IPs are anonymized by GA4 before storage.

Lawful basis

Consent (PDPA s.19, GDPR Art. 6(1)(a)). Analytics scripts are not loaded until you click Accept. Reject blocks all non-essential cookies and trackers.

Cookies and storage

  • cookieConsent in localStorage — strictly necessary, records your Accept/Reject choice so the banner doesn't reappear.
  • theme in localStorage — strictly necessary, remembers light/dark mode preference.
  • GA4 cookies (_ga, _ga_*) — only set after you Accept. You can clear them in your browser at any time.

Your rights

Under PDPA and GDPR you can request access, correction, deletion, or export of any personal data associated with you. Send a request to chairithinug@uwalumni.com and it will be honoured within 30 days.

Data retention

GA4 retention is set to the GA4 default (14 months) and is not enriched with any other identifier. No user-submitted data is stored on the site beyond what is described above.

Contact (Data Protection)

Questions or complaints: chairithinug@uwalumni.com.

Accessibility Statement

This site targets WCAG 2.2 Level AA conformance. The current implementation includes:

  • Skip-to-content link, single H1 per page, semantic landmarks.
  • Keyboard navigation (Tab order, Escape to close sidebar, Arrow keys for carousels).
  • Visible focus indicators on all interactive elements.
  • Color contrast meets the 4.5:1 ratio for body text and 3:1 for UI controls.
  • prefers-reduced-motion respected in CSS and the blob background.
  • All images have alt attributes; flip cards have descriptive aria-label values.
  • Touch targets meet the 44×44 px minimum (WCAG 2.5.8).
  • Profile image served as WebP with JPEG fallback for older browsers.

Known limitations

  • Complex flip-card animations may briefly disorient screen readers; reduced-motion users see static cards.
  • No screen-reader walkthrough has been done with VoiceOver/NVDA yet — feedback welcome.

Feedback

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please email chairithinug@uwalumni.com. I aim to respond within 7 days.
